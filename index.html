OPEN WHEN YOU MISS THESE DAYS

Remember this version of us.

There will be days when these photographs feel like they belong to another lifetime.

People will grow. Dreams will change. Places will change. Everything around us will keep moving.

That's okay.

Because somewhere in this little archive, these moments don't have to change.

They can simply remain exactly as they were.

So whenever life gets too fast, come back here.

Look at a random photograph. Read an old story. Remember the ridiculous things. Remember the good days.

And smile. ❤️
